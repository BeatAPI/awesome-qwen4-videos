## Creator-published prompt

```text
Create a single self-contained HTML file using three.js that renders an ultra-detailed voxel diorama of LONDON on a floating island in the sky. Super high detail, cinematic quality, saturated colors.
ISLAND
Irregular oval floating island (not a perfect circle), wavy organic coastline.
Thick cliff sides with layered rock strata (orange, tan, brown, grey bands), mossy rock outcrops, hanging roots, green vines with small flowers dripping off the rim.
Jagged underside tapering into stalactite-like rock spikes.
The Thames runs across the whole island in turquoise-blue water with light waves, and pours off both edges as waterfalls with mist at the bottom. Add 3–4 smaller waterfalls off the rim.
6 small floating islets around it, each with a tiny prop: a red phone box, a street lamp, a pond, a cottage with a red roof, a flower bed. One islet has its own mini waterfall.

LANDMARKS (instantly recognizable, the main focus; no generic houses or apartment blocks)
Palace of Westminster with gothic spires along the roof, Victoria Tower with a waving Union Jack, and Big Ben (Elizabeth Tower) with 4 working clock faces and moving hands.
London Eye: big white wheel with glass pods, slowly rotating.
Tower Bridge: two stone towers, blue walkways, bascules slowly lifting and lowering.
The Shard (glossy glass pyramid) and the Gherkin (glossy glass bullet with diagonal stripes) on the skyline.
St Paul's Cathedral with a dome; Tower of London (White Tower with 4 turrets and curtain walls).
Buckingham Palace with the golden Victoria Memorial in front, ringed by red flower beds; Queen's Guards in red tunics and black bearskins marching.
Trafalgar Square: Nelson's Column, 4 lions, 2 fountains, the National Gallery behind.
Westminster Bridge with lamps.

PARKS & LIFE
Everything else is lush parkland: striped lawns, winding gravel paths, varied trees (big round plane trees, slim poplars, pink blossom trees), colorful flower beds, lamp posts and red phone boxes along the embankments.
Hundreds of tiny walking people, red double-decker buses and black cabs driving, river boats on the Thames, seagulls flying.
3 hot-air balloons in Union Jack colors drifting around the island; small white voxel clouds.

LIGHTING & RENDER
Sunny late morning: warm low sun with long soft shadows, deep blue gradient sky, bright saturated colors, subtle bloom, ACES tone mapping, ambient occlusion, high-quality antialiasing.

CAMERA & ANIMATION
12-second loop at 30 fps, with motion from the very first frame.
Smooth slow orbit around the island (about 50°) with a very light zoom-in. No close-ups, the whole island always in frame.
Everything animated: wheel turning, bridge lifting, clock hands, buses, boats, people, waterfalls, balloons, islets gently bobbing.
```
