# Rights and attribution

Original recordings, artwork, characters, prompts and referenced assets belong to their respective creators. Each case links its original source. Hosting a viewing copy does not change or grant an underlying media license. The repository's MIT license covers its original scripts and editorial organization only, not third-party media or prompts.

This collection records source-reported Qwen model labels; it does not claim that BeatAPI independently reproduced or authenticated every generation. The sunbeam mascot recording retains the Qoduck watermark and the creator's Max-Preview attribution as an explicit discrepancy, and records the visible paid-partnership disclosure.

For an attribution correction or removal request, open a repository issue with the case ID, original source and requested change. Maintainers should remove both the case link and hosted copies when honoring a removal request.
